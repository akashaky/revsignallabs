FAQ
Common questions
Do I need a tracking pixel or developer to set this up? +
No pixel, no developer, no code. Rev Signal Lab connects to Shopify via OAuth and pulls everything it needs directly from your order and customer data. Setup is entirely self-serve in under 5 minutes.
What if my COGS or ad spend changes over time? +
You can update your cost inputs anytime in Settings. When you change COGS or shipping cost, we recalculate your profit history from the date you specify. Ad spend changes are stored with effective dates so historical profit stays accurate — we never retroactively apply new budgets to old periods.
How far back does Rev Signal Lab sync my data? +
By default we sync the last 12 months of orders on install. This gives you year-over-year comparisons from day one. Scale plan merchants can extend to 24 months. The sync happens in the background — recent data loads first so your dashboard is ready in about 60 seconds.
How is this different from Shopify's built-in analytics? +
Shopify shows you revenue. Rev Signal Lab shows you profit. We subtract your ad spend, COGS, shipping costs, and processing fees to give you net profit and true margin — numbers Shopify doesn't compute. We also show customer LTV, repeat purchase rate, and discount attribution that Shopify has no equivalent for.
How is Rev Signal Lab different from Triple Whale or Polar Analytics? +
Triple Whale and Polar are built around ad attribution — they need a first-party pixel and their value is telling you which Facebook ad drove a purchase. Rev Signal Lab is built around profit clarity and customer retention — no pixel needed, works in 5 minutes, and costs 3–8x less. If you spend $20K+/month on ads and need deep attribution, Triple Whale might be for you. If you want to understand your profit and customers without the complexity, Rev Signal Lab is built for that.

Privacy Policy
How we handle your data
Last updated: April 2025

1. Who we are

Rev Signal Lab ("we", "our", or "us") is a Shopify analytics application. We are the data controller for the information we collect through our app. For questions, contact us at privacy@revsignallab.com.

2. What data we collect

When you install Rev Signal Lab, we access the following data from your Shopify store via the Shopify API:

  • Order data — order amounts, dates, products, discounts, refunds, and fulfilment status
  • Customer data — customer IDs, purchase history, and order frequency (no names or contact details are stored)
  • Product data — product titles, collections, and variants
  • Discount code data — code names, usage counts, and associated order values

We also collect the cost inputs you provide directly (COGS %, shipping cost, ad budget). We do not collect or store payment card details, customer passwords, or personally identifiable customer information such as names, emails, or addresses.

3. How we use your data

We use your store data solely to power your Rev Signal Lab dashboard — calculating net profit, margin, customer analytics, and discount attribution. We do not sell your data, share it with third parties for advertising purposes, or use it to train machine learning models.

4. Data storage and security

Your data is stored on encrypted servers. We use industry-standard security practices including HTTPS for all data in transit and encryption at rest. Access to your store data is restricted to the minimum permissions required to operate the app.

5. Data retention

We retain your store data for as long as your Rev Signal Lab subscription is active. If you uninstall the app, your data is permanently deleted within 30 days. You may request immediate deletion by contacting us at privacy@revsignallab.com.

6. Third-party services

Rev Signal Lab uses the following third-party services to operate:

  • Shopify API — to sync your store data securely via OAuth
  • Cloud hosting provider — to store and process your data
  • Transactional email provider — to deliver your weekly profit email report

Each provider is bound by their own privacy policies and data processing agreements. We do not grant third parties access to your raw store data beyond what is necessary to operate these services.

7. Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete the data we hold about you and your store at any time. To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@revsignallab.com. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify active merchants of material changes via email and update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

9. Contact

For any privacy-related questions or requests, please email us at privacy@revsignallab.com.
