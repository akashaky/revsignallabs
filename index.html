The COD Problem
COD is 60% of Indian
ecommerce — and its
biggest liability

Every undelivered COD order costs you the product, shipping, return shipping, and warehouse handling. For most D2C stores, RTO eats 15–30% of revenue.

Rev Signal Labs sends an automated WhatsApp or SMS message the moment a COD order is placed — and only ships if the customer confirms.

FAQ
Common questions
Does this work with all Shopify plans? +
Yes. Rev Signal Labs works with all Shopify plans including Basic, Shopify, Advanced, and Shopify Plus. Installation is done entirely through the Shopify App Store.
What happens if a customer doesn't reply to WhatsApp? +
We automatically retry via SMS after a configurable waiting period (default: 30 minutes). If there's still no reply after your set number of attempts, the order can be auto-cancelled or flagged for manual review — your choice.
Will this affect my verified/prepaid orders? +
No. Rev Signal Labs only activates for COD orders. Prepaid, UPI, and card orders are completely untouched and fulfil normally through Shopify.
Can I customize the WhatsApp message? +
Yes, on Growth and Scale plans you can fully customize the message template, including your brand name, order details, and CTA text. Starter plan uses our optimized default template.
Is there a free trial? +
Yes — every plan comes with a 14-day free trial. No credit card required. Full access from day one so you can see the RTO reduction for yourself before paying anything.
How do you count "verifications"? +
One verification = one COD order where we send at least one message. It doesn't matter how many retries or channels we use for that order — it counts as one verification in your monthly quota.
